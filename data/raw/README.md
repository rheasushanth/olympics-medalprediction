# Raw data (download these yourself, they are not committed)

Put these files in this folder:

1. `athlete_events.csv` and `noc_regions.csv`
   Kaggle: "120 years of Olympic history: athletes and results"
2. World Development Indicators CSV (World Bank, worldbank.org)
   Must include the year 2016, since 2016 is our test year.
   Needed indicators: GDP, GDP per capita, GDP growth, total population,
   population growth, land area.

Check after downloading: the WDI file has data up to 2016 or later.
